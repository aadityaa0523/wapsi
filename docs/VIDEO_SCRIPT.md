|---|---|---|

| 0:12 | RBI paragraph 76T on the RBI site | "From 1 January 2027, that can cost you your compensation. RBI's rule needs a report to both the bank and the police route, within five days." |
| 0:28 | Wapsi first question | "Wapsi is built for that frightened moment. One question at a time." |
| 0:35 | Click "No, it already happened", "Today", enter 10000, "Continue", "Yes" | "When, how much, and did you share an OTP. No forms, no legal words." |
| 0:50 | Clocks appear | "Two clocks. The money at stake. One next action." |
| 1:00 | Turn on "I told my bank" | "Now I've told my bank. Most people would stop here. Wapsi says: one report is not enough." |

| 1:20 | More help, My deadline, agent running on a story (only if the key is connected) | "Here the AI reads the story in the victim's own words and chooses its steps. But plain code decides every date and amount, and a fact checker rejects any invented number." |
